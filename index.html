Long Screenshot

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 24, 2026 · macOS app · bundle ID oneclick.screenshot.jp

Summary

Long Screenshot is a local macOS utility. It captures a screen region you select, stitches scrolling frames into one PNG, and saves that file to a folder you choose. The app does not create accounts, does not include analytics or advertising SDKs, and is sandboxed without network entitlements. We do not collect, sell, or transmit your personal information or screenshot contents to our servers — because the app has no servers to send them to.

Information the app uses on your Mac

  • Screen Recording. When you start a capture, macOS may ask for Screen Recording permission. The app uses Apple’s ScreenCaptureKit to record only the display region you select. Frames stay on your Mac, are processed locally (alignment and stitching), and are never uploaded.
  • Exported images. The finished PNG is written only to the folder you authorize through the system folder picker. The app stores a security-scoped bookmark for that folder so it can save later captures.
  • Temporary files. Capture tiles are stored in the app’s temporary directory during a session and are deleted after export or cancellation.
  • Preferences. Your keyboard shortcut and save-folder bookmark are stored locally in standard UserDefaults on your Mac.

The app does not access iCloud, Contacts, Photos library, Location, Camera, Microphone, or Accessibility APIs.

What we do not collect

  • No names, emails, IDs, or account data
  • No analytics, crash reporters, or advertising identifiers
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No screenshot contents sent off-device

Third parties

The app does not share data with third parties. Distribution through the Mac App Store is handled by Apple under Apple’s own policies.

Children

Long Screenshot does not target children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
